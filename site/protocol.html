01

Cryptographic Content Substrate

SHA-256 Word, Paragraph & Chapter Hashing
Deterministic Pipeline

At the base of the protocol lies the deterministic compiler. Every single word, paragraph, and chapter written by Kidd James is stripped of platform artifacts and hashed using lowercase SHA-256 digests.

Five distinct Merkle trees are constructed per edition:

  • Manuscript Tree: Ordered text blocks in canonical reading sequence.
  • Artifact Tree: Embedded contracts, emails, SWIFT MT799 records, and IMFPA agreements.
  • Image Tree: Chapter covers and visual assets.
  • Prompt Tree: The raw text prompts utilized for AI generation provenance.
  • Audio Tree: AI-narrated chapters and story audio files.
// Composite Edition Root
editionRoot = SHA-256(
  manuscriptRoot ||
  artifactRoot ||
  imageRoot ||
  promptRoot ||
  audioRoot
);

02

On-Chain Registry & State Machine

PublishingKernel & LiteraryAnchor on Polygon Mainnet
Polygon · Chain ID 137

The on-chain kernel acts as the permanent legal and cryptographic registry. The contracts are non-upgradeable and contain zero admin override keys.

Editions obey a strict forward-only state machine:

  • Anchored: Edition root committed to Polygon Mainnet.
  • Canonical: Designated as the official authoritative edition.
  • Superseded: Replaced by a newer version (history preserved forever).
  • Retracted: Explicitly marked with an on-chain justification string.

03

Storage & Content Addressing

IPFS Content Identifier (CID) Pinning
Decentralized Storage

Raw manuscript markdown, embedded artifacts, and full-resolution audio files are published to IPFS.

Because IPFS addresses content exclusively by its cryptographic hash (CID), it is physically impossible for hosting providers to replace text, silently edit sentences, or alter historical editions.

04

Cross-Chain Temporal Anchoring

Bitcoin OpenTimestamps Attestation
Bitcoin Proof

To prevent reliance on any single consensus network, edition roots are cross-anchored into the Bitcoin blockchain via OpenTimestamps.

This establishes indisputable chronological priority, proving the manuscript existed in its exact byte-for-byte state prior to the timestamped Bitcoin block height.

OTS Engine: OpenTimestamps v0.4.1
Attestation: BTC Block Header Merkle Path
Verification: ots verify edition-ots.ots
