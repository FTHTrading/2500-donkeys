Architecture

The novel is built in four narrative layers, each expanding the world of phantom finance and real consequences.

Layer A — Character Depth

Raymond's first deal. Gerald's parking lot. Marcus's connection. The Storyteller's vault. Philippe's Geneva WeWork. The Seven Families who control everything and nothing.

Layer B — WhatsApp Mutations

Lagos, Dubai, Zurich, NGO aid-washing, carbon conference. The same deal, forwarded through five ecosystems, each adding a layer of plausibility.

Layer C — The Procession

2,500 donkeys. 34 days across the Sahel. Ibrahim walks. Adama shares water. 653 donkeys don't arrive. The certificate says they all did.

Layer D — Aftermath

Raymond goes home. The WhatsApp groups die. The Storyteller speaks at a London summit. And somewhere, a yak-backed lithium corridor is born.
