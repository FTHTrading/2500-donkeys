# Style Guide — The 2,500 Donkeys

## Voice & Tone

- **Narrator voice:** Calm, controlled, intelligent, observational. Think Morgan Freeman narrating a financial autopsy.
- **Never:** Angry, preachy, conspiratorial, ranty, or accusatory.
- **The satire works through:** Juxtaposition, understatement, and letting absurdity speak for itself.
- **Commentary style:** One precise sentence. Never a paragraph-long moral lecture.

### Example of correct tone:
> "The deal expanded in inverse proportion to its certainty."

### Example of wrong tone:
> "This is what happens when greedy brokers exploit the system and lie to everyone involved."

---

## Prose Rules

1. **Short paragraphs.** Rarely more than 4 sentences.
2. **Scene-first.** Open with physical detail (condensation on windshield, parking lot, phone glow).
3. **Dialogue is sparse.** When brokers speak, it's clipped, jargon-heavy, performative.
4. **No exclamation marks in prose.** (WhatsApp artifacts are the exception — those are ALL CAPS URGENCY.)
5. **Numbers are written as numerals** in broker/finance contexts: 500MT, 12%, $440 million.
6. **Repetition is a tool.** "The commission waterfall was finalized" should recur as a refrain.

---

## Vocabulary

### Words to use freely:
- Ecosystem, structure, corridor, throughput, lifecycle, optimization
- Mandate, compliance, alignment, settlement, waterfall
- Deterministic, canonical, immutable, provenance

### Words to avoid in prose:
- Scam, fraud, criminal, evil, corrupt (let readers conclude this themselves)
- Obviously, clearly, of course (don't tell readers what's obvious)
- Ironically (show the irony, don't label it)

---

## Artifact Style

### IMFPA / NCNDA documents:
- Formal legal language, slightly overwrought
- Placeholders left unfilled as satire (e.g., "[PARTY B NAME HERE]")
- Clauses that are logically absurd but tonally straight-faced

### WhatsApp forwards:
- ALL CAPS for urgency
- Emoji as punctuation: 🚨 ✅ 🏆 💎 🇨🇭 🇦🇪 🇬🇧
- Each forward escalates the previous claims
- "Forwarded many times" tag

### ESG deck / Carbon docs:
- Corporate slide deck language
- Real-sounding metrics with footnotes revealing they're fictional
- SDG alignment tables that quietly contradict themselves

### Commission waterfall:
- Clean table format
- Roles multiply with each revision
- Total always exceeds logical limits
- Notes at bottom reveal the absurdity

## Character Voice Notes

### Raymond
- Alpha broker. Speaks in declarations.
- Never asks questions. Answers questions with non-answers.
- "The file is the product."

### Marcus
- Connector. References "my guy" constantly.
- Dubai-adjacent. Never physically present. Always on speaker.

### Gerald
- Peripheral. Quiet. Eats fast food during meetings.
- Gets the smallest commission. Always accepts it.
- Sends 👍 in response to everything.

### Philippe
- Geneva WeWork. Spreadsheets as worldview.
- Has never touched the product (gold or donkey).
- Adjusts denominators rather than confronting failure.

### The Storyteller
- Observer. Architect of the donkey experiment.
- Never participates in the deal. Only watches.
- Speaks in precise, measured sentences.

## Structural Rules

1. Each chapter is a "Block" — referencing blockchain blocks.
2. Artifact inserts appear between chapters as "Exhibits."
3. The frame device: a recovered IPFS archive.
4. The opening and closing reference the Genesis hash.
5. Circular structure: ends where it begins (parking lot, new deal, cycle resets).

---

## The One Rule That Governs Everything

**Show behavior. Never declare conspiracy.**

The satire lands when readers recognize the pattern themselves.
If you have to explain why something is absurd, it isn't absurd enough yet.
