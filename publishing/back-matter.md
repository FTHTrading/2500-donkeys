## Glossary of Terms

The world of commodity brokerage has its own language. Some of these terms are real, used daily across thousands of WhatsApp groups and deal rooms. Some have been adapted for this novel. All of them function the same way in fiction as they do in practice: as architecture for belief.

**AU Doré** — Unrefined gold bars, typically 85–92% pure, produced at mine sites before being sent to a refinery. In WhatsApp deals, "AU doré at discount" is the opening line of approximately ten thousand concurrent transactions worldwide, most of which reference product that cannot be independently verified.

**CIF (Cost, Insurance, and Freight)** — An international shipping term indicating the seller delivers goods to a named port and covers shipping and insurance costs. In this ecosystem, "CIF Rotterdam" is a magic phrase. It means nothing has shipped, but it sounds like it could.

**Commission Waterfall** — The layered structure of fees paid to intermediaries upon deal closure. In a typical commodity deal, the waterfall might be 2–3% split among a few parties. In the deals depicted here, the waterfall exceeds the GDP of small nations and includes line items for roles that do not exist.

**ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance)** — A framework for evaluating a company's ethical and sustainability practices. In this novel, ESG language is deployed as a narrative accelerant — a way to make any proposition sound not merely profitable but *virtuous*.

**IMFPA (Irrevocable Master Fee Protection Agreement)** — A document used in commodity brokerage to protect intermediaries' commissions. It is typically signed before the deal closes and specifies each party's percentage. In this novel, the IMFPA reproduces itself. Each version generates addenda. Each addendum generates side letters. The paper is the product.

**IPFS (InterPlanetary File System)** — A peer-to-peer protocol for storing and sharing files in a distributed network. Content is addressed by its cryptographic hash (CID), making it permanently verifiable and immutable. This novel was pinned to IPFS before publication.

**LBMA Fix** — The London Bullion Market Association gold price, set twice daily as the global benchmark for gold transactions. "Below LBMA fix" is the siren call of every WhatsApp gold deal — a discount on a price that is publicly verifiable, offered by sellers who are privately unverifiable.

**Mandate** — A person authorized to act on behalf of a buyer or seller. In practice, mandate chains can extend through dozens of intermediaries, each claiming proximity to the principal that none of them have met.

**NCNDA (Non-Circumvention, Non-Disclosure Agreement)** — A contract preventing parties from bypassing intermediaries in a deal. Everyone signs one. No one reads the one they sign. They sign it because the architecture requires it.

**Narrative Density** — A term used in this novel to describe the threshold at which a deal becomes self-sustaining. When enough layers of documentation, terminology, and forwarded messages accumulate, the deal no longer requires a product. The narrative *is* the product.

**POF (Proof of Funds)** — Documentation proving a buyer has the financial capacity to complete a transaction. "Show POF" is the most common demand in WhatsApp deal groups. It is also the most commonly deferred.

**SPA (Sale and Purchase Agreement)** — The definitive contract for the actual sale of goods. In the ecosystem, the SPA is always forthcoming, never arriving, and perpetually "being reviewed by counsel."

**Soft Probe / Hard Probe** — Banking verification procedures. A "soft probe" is an informal inquiry into an account's existence. A "hard probe" is a formal verification of funds. Both are invoked constantly. Neither is performed.

**The Storyteller** — Not a broker. Not a buyer. Not a seller. Someone who listened to the grammar long enough to understand it, and then tested it. A figure who exists at the edge of the ecosystem, observing its mechanics without participating in its rewards.

**URDG 758** — Uniform Rules for Demand Guarantees, published by the International Chamber of Commerce. Referenced in every IMFPA for gravitas. Understood by few. Read by fewer.
