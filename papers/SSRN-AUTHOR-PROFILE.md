## Academic Bio (for "About" section)

```
Kevan Burns is the founder of FTH Trading and an independent researcher 
focused on deterministic publishing infrastructure and digital provenance 
systems. His work addresses the structural problem of manuscript verification 
— establishing when a document existed, in what exact form, and who authored 
it — using cryptographic primitives rather than institutional trust.

Burns designed and deployed the Literary Protocol Standard (LPS-1), a 
five-layer provenance architecture combining Merkle tree hashing, 
content-addressed storage, on-chain anchoring, and cross-chain timestamping. 
The reference implementation accompanies a 75,000-word literary work and 
is deployed on Polygon mainnet, with all smart contracts source-verified 
and independently auditable.

His research interests include reproducible build pipelines, content 
integrity verification, programmable rights management, and the intersection 
of cryptographic infrastructure with creative publishing.

Research areas: Digital Provenance, Deterministic Publishing, Content 
Integrity, Blockchain Infrastructure, Intellectual Property Systems.
```
